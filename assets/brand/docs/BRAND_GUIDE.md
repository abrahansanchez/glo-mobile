# Glō Brand Guide

## Positioning
Glō is a premium, minimal AI receptionist brand built for service businesses across industries.
The brand should remain neutral and professional, not barber-only.

## Core Marks
- Primary mark: glowing circle + `Glō` wordmark.
- Icon mark: glowing circle only (no text).

## Theme Usage
- Dark mode: preferred default treatment.
- Light mode: use safe variants that reduce/invert glow intensity while preserving contrast and legibility.

## Do Not
- Do not use barber pole imagery.
- Do not use scissors, combs, clippers, or niche trade symbols.
- Do not add extra decorative symbols that narrow the brand to one vertical.

## Color Guidance
- Primary look: neon blue glow on black background.
- Keep palette restrained and minimal.
- Avoid adding extra accent colors unless required for accessibility.

## Export List And Placement
Place final exports into these folders:

- `assets/brand/logo/`
  - `glo_logo.png`
  - `glo_logo_vector.svg`
  - `glo_website_logo.png`

- `assets/brand/icon/`
  - `glo_social_icon.png`
  - `glo_favicon.png`
  - `app_icon.png`

- `assets/brand/splash/`
  - `splash_universal.png`
  - `splash_iphone.png`
  - `splash_android.png`
  - `splash_tablet.png`

- `assets/brand/animations/`
  - `splash_animation_reference.gif`

## Notes
Keep source-of-truth brand files in this `assets/brand/` kit and copy finalized runtime assets into app-configured paths as needed.
